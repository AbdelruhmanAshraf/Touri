# Alexandria Travel Guide

## Overview
Alexandria is Egypt's second-largest city and its main port, stretching along the Mediterranean coast for 32km. Founded by Alexander the Great in 331 BC, it was once the intellectual capital of the ancient world.

## Must-See Attractions
- **Qaitbay Citadel** — 15th-century fortress built on the site of the ancient Lighthouse of Alexandria
- **Bibliotheca Alexandrina** — Modern library on the site of the ancient Library of Alexandria; stunning architecture
- **Alexandria National Museum** — Three floors of artifacts from pharaonic to modern times
- **Montaza Palace & Gardens** — Royal summer palace surrounded by beautiful gardens on the sea
- **Ras el-Tin Palace** — Another royal palace open to visitors
- **El Alamein War Museum** — WWII memorial 105km west of Alexandria
- **Pompey's Pillar** — Ancient Roman column in the Serapeum district
- **Catacombs of Kom el-Shoqafa** — Roman-Egyptian burial site with unique hybrid art

## Corniche & Sea Life
- The 32km Corniche promenade is perfect for walking and cycling
- Local beach clubs accessible in summer
- Seafood is exceptional — best in Egypt

## Dining
- **Fish Market** — Best seafood in Egypt, choose your fresh fish and watch it cooked
- **Kadoura** — Legendary seafood institution since 1967
- **Mohamed Ahmed** — Famous for fool (fava beans) and traditional Egyptian breakfast
- **Zephyrion** — Waterfront seafood restaurant in Abu Qir

## Getting Around
- **Tram** — Historic tramway system (EGP 2-5), great for the Corniche and central areas
- **Minibus** — Cheap and frequent
- **Taxi** — Rideshare apps (Uber/Careem) available

## Best Time to Visit
- **April-June and September-October**: Ideal (22-28°C, not crowded)
- **July-August**: Hot and crowded with Egyptian summer tourists
- **Winter**: Cool and quiet, some sea activities closed

## Day Trips from Alexandria
- **El Alamein** — WWII battlefield and cemetery (2 hours west)
- **Abu Mena** — Early Christian city, UNESCO World Heritage Site
- **Rosetta** — Historic city with Nile delta scenery

## Budget Estimates (per day, per person)
- Economy: 350-600 EGP
- Mid-range: 900-1,500 EGP
- Luxury: 2,000-4,000 EGP

## Practical Tips
- Book accommodation early in summer (July-August is peak season)
- Seafood is fresher and cheaper in Abu Qir (15km east)
- Women should dress modestly outside beach areas
- Water taxi across the harbor is a unique local experience
