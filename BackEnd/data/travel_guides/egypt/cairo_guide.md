# Cairo Travel Guide

## Overview
Cairo is Egypt's vibrant capital city, home to over 20 million people and thousands of years of history. It sits along the Nile River and is the gateway to iconic ancient wonders.

## Must-See Attractions
- **Pyramids of Giza** — The last surviving wonder of the ancient world. Visit at sunrise for the best experience.
- **Egyptian Museum** — Home to Tutankhamun's golden mask and over 120,000 artifacts.
- **Sphinx** — The iconic limestone monument guarding the Giza plateau.
- **Khan el-Khalili** — A bustling medieval bazaar perfect for souvenirs, spices, and local crafts.
- **Al-Azhar Mosque** — One of the oldest universities in the world, built in 970 AD.
- **Citadel of Saladin** — Medieval fortress with stunning views of Cairo.
- **Coptic Cairo** — Ancient Christian quarter with the Hanging Church and Ben Ezra Synagogue.

## Neighborhoods
- **Zamalek** — Upscale island district with cafes, galleries, and embassies
- **Downtown/Wust el-Balad** — Historic center with colonial architecture
- **Maadi** — Quiet residential area popular with expats
- **New Cairo** — Modern planned city with malls and restaurants

## Dining
- **Koshary** is Cairo's beloved street food — lentils, rice, macaroni, and spicy tomato sauce (EGP 15-30)
- **Felfela** — Famous traditional Egyptian restaurant in Downtown
- **Sequence** — Upscale rooftop dining
- **Andrea El Mariouteya** — Classic Egyptian food in a garden setting

## Getting Around
- Metro: Fast, cheap (EGP 8-15 per trip), covers key tourist areas
- Uber/Careem: Most convenient for tourists
- Taxi: Negotiate price beforehand
- Microbuses: Very cheap, but complex routes for visitors

## Best Time to Visit
- **October to April**: Ideal weather (15-25°C)
- **Summer** (June-August): Very hot (35-40°C), avoid midday sun
- **Ramadan**: Unique cultural experience but restaurant hours change

## Practical Tips
- Dress modestly when visiting religious sites
- Bargain respectfully in bazaars (start at 40% of asking price)
- Carry small change (EGP coins) for tips
- Photography is generally allowed but ask permission for people
- Water from taps is not safe to drink — buy bottled water

## Budget Estimates (per day, per person)
- Economy: 400-700 EGP
- Mid-range: 1,000-2,000 EGP  
- Luxury: 3,000-6,000 EGP
