# Luxor Travel Guide

## Overview
Luxor is an open-air museum — arguably the world's greatest concentration of ancient monuments. Located 670km south of Cairo along the Nile, it was the ancient capital of Thebes.

## Must-See Attractions
- **Valley of the Kings** — Rock-cut tombs of pharaohs including Tutankhamun. Book in advance.
- **Karnak Temple Complex** — Largest temple complex in the world, a 30-minute walk of grandeur.
- **Luxor Temple** — Illuminated beautifully at night; free to walk around the exterior.
- **Temple of Hatshepsut** — Stunning mortuary temple carved into cliffs at Deir el-Bahari.
- **Colossi of Memnon** — Two giant statues of Amenhotep III standing 18 meters tall.
- **Medinet Habu** — Often overlooked but one of the best-preserved temples in Egypt.

## Nile Experiences
- **Felucca ride** — Sail the Nile at sunset (EGP 50-150/hour, negotiate)
- **Hot air balloon** — Sunrise balloon ride over the Valley of the Kings (USD 80-150)
- **Nile cruise** — Multi-day cruises between Luxor and Aswan

## Getting Around
- **Bicycle** — The East Bank is very flat and bike-friendly (EGP 50-100/day rental)
- **Calèche (horse carriage)** — Traditional but negotiate price firmly
- **Minibus** — Cheap but infrequent
- **Taxi** — Negotiate beforehand

## Dining
- **Sofra Restaurant** — Authentic Egyptian food in a beautiful old house
- **Chez Omar** — Popular with locals, excellent kofta
- **1886 Restaurant at Winter Palace** — Upscale dining in a historic hotel
- **Al-Sahaby Lane** — Multiple budget-friendly Egyptian options

## Best Time to Visit
- **October to February**: Perfect weather (20-28°C daytime)
- **Summer**: Extremely hot (40-45°C) — arrive very early to monuments

## Practical Tips
- Combine East Bank (temples) and West Bank (tombs) itineraries
- Hire a licensed guide for Valley of the Kings — they know which tombs are currently open
- Purchase a photography permit inside monuments
- Many sites close mid-afternoon — plan early morning visits

## Budget Estimates (per day, per person)
- Economy: 350-600 EGP (excluding entry fees)
- Mid-range: 900-1,800 EGP
- Luxury: 2,500-5,000 EGP
- Entry fees: Valley of Kings EGP 400+, Karnak EGP 200
